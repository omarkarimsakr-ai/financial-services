The future of asset management: Azimut Egypt compared with Liontrust.

Introduction

The sector, why we chose it and the two companies.

Mobilise savings

Turns small individual savings into professionally managed pools.

Allocate capital

Channels money to companies, governments and projects through capital markets.

Spread risk

Gives savers diversification, easy access to their money and regulated protection.

Asset managers invest household, pension and institutional money in equities, bonds, gold and property. The FRA supervises Egyptian fund managers and the FCA does so in the UK.

Why these two countries

Egypt: emerging and fast-moving

EGP 471bnfund net assets, June 2026 (+14.7% in a quarter)
79%of people aged 15+ hold an active financial account
~1%of GDP in mutual funds in 2019, so room to grow is large

UK: mature and global

£11.1tnmanaged in the UK in 2025 (+11% on 2024)
53%of UK-managed assets belong to overseas clients
35%of UK adults invest, so participation can still grow

The two companies

Azimut Egypt

Part of Azimut Group (Italy); entered Egypt by acquiring Rasmala Egypt in 2019.

Scale. About EGP 49bn AUM in Egypt (June 2026); the parent group manages €157.9bn.

Products. AZ-Savings, AZ Equity-Egypt, AZ-Opp, AZ Gold, Halan AZ Real Estate Fund and the azinvest platform.

Position. First in Egyptian asset management to win an FRA fintech licence (azinvest, 2024).

Liontrust

Independent, London-listed fund manager, founded 1994.

Scale. £24.3bn assets under management and advice at 30 June 2026, including £3.0bn from River Global.

Products. Sustainable Future, Economic Advantage, Global Innovation, Cashflow Solution, multi-asset funds and the Edinburgh Investment Trust.

Position. Net outflows of £4.2bn in FY2026, with flows improving as international institutional sales grow.

Industry and market analysis

Size, structure, inclusion and digital adoption.

Egypt: investment funds

$9.3bn

EGP 470.9bn of net assets at June 2026, up 14.7% in one quarter and 49% since December 2025 (EGP 316bn).

224 funds; individuals hold 74.7% of fund units. In 2019 funds were about 1% of GDP, over 90% in money market and fixed income.

UK: assets under management

£11.1tn

Managed in 2025, up 11% from £10.0tn. £5.9tn (53%, up from 37% in 2016) is managed for overseas clients.

Retail is now 30% of AUM, ahead of pensions at 25%. PwC projects £16tn by 2030.

Market structure and service offerings

Egypt is a retail-led domestic savings market; the UK is a global export hub where retail is only 30% of assets.

Financial inclusion: two different questions

Egypt: can people reach an account?

79%of people aged 15+ hold an active account (56.4m)
72.5%of women, up from 19.1% in 2016
58%of people aged 15 to 35, up from 36.3% in 2020

UK: do people with accounts invest?

35%of UK adults invest (FCA Financial Lives 2024)
22.6madults subscribed to an ISA in 2023/24
23mconsumers the FCA sees as underserved by advice and guidance

Egypt’s gap is access and usage; the UK’s gap is confidence and advice. Same mission, different starting lines. The two measures are not directly comparable.

Digital adoption in investment products

Egypt: rules opened the door

Rules. FRA decisions 139 to 141 of 2022 and the Fintech Law enable e-KYC and digital onboarding.

Reach. Thndr reports 503,000+ mutual fund investors and EGP 10bn of fund assets.

Scope. Apps distribute fractional real estate and gold funds.

UK: policy builds confidence

Platforms. Non-advised platform use has risen since February 2020, when it was 5.9%.

Policy. Targeted Support began on 6 April 2026.

Products. The Long-Term Asset Fund framework is opening private markets to retail savers.

Digital transformation and technology

Programmes, infrastructure, cyber security and trust.

Egypt’s transformation is front-end and access-led; Liontrust’s is back-end and efficiency-led. Each has what the other lacks.

What digital infrastructure delivers

Cyber security, data protection and digital trust

Egypt’s regime

Data law. Law 151/2020 requires consent, breach notification and a DPO; full enforcement is due late 2026.

FRA Decree 227/2025. Licensed firms file IT, cyber and risk policies and run annual tests.

Safekeeping. Funds use custodian banks; AZ Gold is held at EGY Cash, a CBE subsidiary.

UK’s regime

Resilience. Existing FCA and Bank of England rules apply to cyber risk.

Accountability. Under SM&CR a named senior manager answers for cyber and AI risk.

Readiness gap. Only 10% of organisations feel ready for AI-augmented cyber threats.

Neither firm discloses technical controls publicly, so we compare the regimes each operates under.

Critical analysis

Strategies, FinTech and AI, products and alignment.

Strategy: breadth versus depth

Azimut Egypt

Strategy. Wide shelf from money market to gold and real estate; five new funds planned for 2026.

Delivery. Partner apps and brokers; Azimut Group backs growth.

Momentum. Sector NAV up 14.7% in Q2 2026; AUM up about EGP 14bn since January.

Liontrust

Strategy. Active, process-led specialist teams.

Delivery. Adviser, platform and institutional sales; bolt-ons such as River Global.

Momentum. Net outflows narrowed to £357m in the quarter to June 2026, with £520m of gross institutional inflows.

Opposite trajectories, shared mission

Azimut Egypt AUM, EGP bn

Hover or tap a point. About 5.8 times the Rasmala base it bought in 2018.

Liontrust AUMA, £bn

Hover or tap a point. 35% below December 2021, even after adding £3.0bn from River Global.

FinTech, AI and emerging technology

Azimut Egypt uses FinTech to widen access; Liontrust uses AI to raise efficiency. The open gap is AI at Azimut Egypt, where the FRA already permits robo-advice.

Innovative products and services

Azimut Egypt

AZ Gold. Egypt’s first Sharia-compliant gold fund, backed 100% by 24-karat gold held at EGY Cash.

Halan AZ Real Estate Fund. First digitally distributed real estate fund: buy in-app, earn rental income, trade the stake.

azinvest. One-click access to money market, fixed income, equity, multi-currency and gold funds.

Liontrust

Sustainable Future. Sustainability-led equity and bond funds across UK and Irish ranges.

Global Innovation and Technology. Thematic equity strategies; GF Global Technology launched recently.

Cashflow Solution and River Global. Income strategies plus multi-style and recovery funds added in June 2026.

Where Azimut Egypt aligns, differs and could borrow

Aligns

Both grow by partnership and acquisition: Rasmala (2019) and River Global (2026). Both work with regulators that back digital change and demand resilience. Both serve retail and institutional clients.

Differs

Scale: about US$1bn in Egypt versus £24.3bn in the UK. Reach: app-led retail savers versus adviser and institution-led clients. Momentum: Azimut Egypt AUM is rising; Liontrust is stabilising after outflows.

Could borrow

Azimut Egypt from Liontrust: published performance rankings, firm-wide AI and data governance, institutional sales abroad.

Liontrust from Azimut Egypt: digital onboarding and low-ticket products through fintech partners.

Conclusion and recommendations

Findings and practical next steps.

Scale versus speed

The UK industry is £11.1tn and global. Egypt’s fund assets are about US$9.3bn but grew 49% in six months.

Access versus advice

Egypt’s gap is accounts and usage, with 79% included. The UK’s gap is confidence: only 35% of adults invest.

Digital: rules versus resilience

Azimut Egypt uses FRA fintech licences to reach retail savers. Liontrust uses AI and data to improve efficiency and outcomes.

Breadth versus depth

Azimut Egypt widens the shelf with gold, real estate and Islamic funds. Liontrust deepens active expertise and consolidates.

Recommendations for Azimut Egypt and the sector

One mission: make investing work for more people

Egypt shows how fast digital access can scale. The UK shows how deep expertise and resilience sustain it. Together they point to the future of asset management.
